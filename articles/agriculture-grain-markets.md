## Market context

Commodity markets are shaped by physical production, infrastructure, transport, financial conditions and the decisions of market participants. A real article on this topic could introduce the relevant market, explain how it works and distinguish established facts from the author's own analysis.

## What a full BSCS article could include

A published report could present a clearly defined research question, evidence from cited data, charts or tables, and a concise interpretation of the findings. The goal would be to explain not only what changed in the market but also **why it matters** for producers, traders, investors or consumers.
